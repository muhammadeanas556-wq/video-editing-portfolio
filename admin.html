Videos stay on Instagram, YouTube or TikTok — only the link is saved, so no video storage is needed. Links are stored in your site's Netlify Blobs store and every visitor's browser loads them from there. Instagram and TikTok posts must be public for the player to work. Poster images are optional: YouTube and TikTok covers are added automatically; for Instagram, add a poster so the card isn't blank. Uploading a poster needs CLOUDINARY_CLOUD_NAME and CLOUDINARY_UPLOAD_PRESET in Netlify; you can always paste a poster image link instead.
